Two boys, Rahul and Aman, were good friends who loved playing computer games. They had many common interests and enjoyed spending time together. However, they had only one computer at home, so they had to share it. Every evening, both boys wanted to use the PC at the same time. Rahul wanted to play a game, while Aman wanted to watch videos and complete his schoolwork. Sometimes they argued about who should use the computer first. One day, their mother noticed them fighting and explained the importance of sharing and cooperation. The boys decided to make a timetable. Rahul would use the PC for one hour, and then Aman would use it for the next hour. After following the timetable, their arguments stopped. They learned that sharing the computer made things easier and fairer for both of them. They also started helping each other with games and school projects. The two boys understood that friendship is more important than winning an argument. Having one PC was no longer a problem because they learned to share and cooperate. The story teaches us that sharing, patience, and teamwork can solve many problems.
